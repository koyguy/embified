  2. Why a card? Oracle uses it only to check you’re a real person. You type it on Oracle’s page — Embified never sees or stores card details.

    What to expect

    • 💳 Oracle places a temporary authorization of about US$1 (or the equivalent in your currency) and reverses it right away. Your bank decides how fast it disappears from your statement — usually a few days.
    • 🔐 Your bank may show an OTP / 3-D Secure screen. That’s normal.
    • 🆓 Always Free is never charged. Your card is only charged if you yourself upgrade to Pay As You Go — you don’t need to.
    • ✅ Accepted: credit cards, and debit cards that work like credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex). ❌ Not accepted: prepaid, virtual/single-use, or PIN-only debit cards.

    🇮🇳 India checklist (do this first)

    • Turn on International transactions and Online / e-commerce transactions for the card in your bank app — many Indian cards have them off by default.
    • Check the card has enough limit/balance for the small temporary hold.
    Card declined or stuck? Troubleshooting
    • “Error processing transaction”: enable international + online transactions, make sure name and address match the card, or try a credit card instead of a debit card.
    • Several declines in a row: stop and wait a few hours — repeated attempts can get flagged. Then try once more, ideally with a different card.
    • “Account is being provisioned”: normal. Wait for Oracle’s “your account is ready” email before signing in.
    • Signup rejected: Oracle’s checks are their own. Use the chat on Oracle’s signup page, or take the Home box path (no card).

    Source: Oracle docs — card authorization at Free Tier signup · Oracle Free Tier FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

        
Is it really free?
Yes. The vault runs on Oracle Cloud’s Always Free resources (an Arm VM + up to 200 GB of block storage), which have no time limit. Embified itself is open source and free. Oracle also gives a 30-day trial credit — you don’t need it.

        
What does Oracle charge?
Nothing while you stay within Always Free. At signup Oracle temporarily authorizes about US$1 (or local equivalent) to verify your card and reverses it; your bank may take a few days to release it. Your card is only charged if you choose to upgrade to Pay As You Go and use paid services. Our stack uses default sizes that fit inside Always Free.

        
Why does Oracle need my card?
Identity verification, to stop abuse of free accounts. You enter it on Oracle’s site only — Embified never sees, collects or stores card details.

        
Can Oracle take my free server away?
Oracle may reclaim Always Free VMs that look idle for a long time. Embified installs an “idle-reclaim shield” that keeps light, legitimate activity (a periodic digest + health ping). Back up anything critical.

        
Who can read my chats?
Only you. Messages and media are stored on your VM’s disk, behind your password. Embified has no central server holding your chats.

        
Linked device (Baileys) vs official Cloud API?
Linked device works like WhatsApp Web and captures groups you’re in, but it uses an unofficial client — WhatsApp could restrict numbers that misuse such clients, so use it gently for archiving. The official Cloud API is sanctioned by Meta but needs a business number and setup, and group support depends on eligibility.

        
I don’t have a credit card.
Try a debit card with international transactions enabled (not prepaid/virtual), or use the Home box path on any always-on computer.

        
Can I stop halfway?
Yes. Your progress is saved in this browser — come back to this page any time and press Continue.
